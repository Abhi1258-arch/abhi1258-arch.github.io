About

I like understanding why things work, and what happens when you test them.

I’m interested in mathematics, economics and markets, and equally in music, film and the small details of ordinary life: the light on a wall in the afternoon, the pause before someone answers a question.

On the numbers side, that has meant backtesting simple trading rules on Indian stocks and reporting it honestly when they lost to the index, writing papers on inflation, digital payments, monetary policy and investor psychology, keeping a weekly market journal, and starting a dashboard for Indian markets.

On the other side, it has meant two poems, three original guitar riffs, and a feature screenplay about a young man who tries to understand other people’s lives through a camera.

I don’t think of these as two halves. Both start with noticing something and wanting to know how it works.
